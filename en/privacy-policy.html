Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 24, 2026

1. Who administers the site

The site 15057.org.ua is administered by the NGO “The Future Has the Right”, Kyiv. The owner of personal data received through the site is the NGO “The Future Has the Right”. Contact for requests regarding personal data: office@15057.org.ua.

2. What data we receive

When a statement of endorsement is submitted, the following data may be processed:

  • surname, first name, patronymic;
  • postal address, telephone number and e-mail;
  • position, status and name of the organization - if available;
  • signed statement, signature image or electronic signature file;
  • records of the consents provided;
  • technical information required for the form to work: date and time of submission, page address, name, file format and size.

Please do not include a child’s personal data, medical records or any information that is not required to endorse the resolution.

3. Why the data is processed

  • verifying the authenticity and completeness of the statement;
  • recording endorsement of the public resolution regarding Draft Law No. 15057;
  • maintaining a register of statements and a list of signatories;
  • communication with the applicant regarding clarification of data or progress of the initiative;
  • submission and public presentation of the resolution to state authorities;
  • protection of the site and form from technical abuse.

4. Consent and Publication

Data is processed on the basis of the person’s voluntary consent given before the form is submitted. Only the person’s full name, position or status, and organisation may be published in the list of signatories, and only where the person has expressly consented.

Postal address, telephone number, email address, signature and the statement file itself are not published.

5. Where the data is stored and to whom it may be transferred

Statements and registry entries are stored in private Google Drive and Google Sheets resources and transmitted through Google Apps Script. Cloudflare may provide site infrastructure and traffic protection. As a result, technical processing may take place on servers outside Ukraine under the respective providers’ terms.

Only persons authorised to administer the initiative and verify endorsements have access to the statements. Data is not sold or shared with advertisers.

6. Storage period

Private statement files and contact details are retained for as long as necessary to carry out and document the initiative, but generally for no longer than three years after the campaign concerning Draft Law No. 15057 ends. They may be retained longer where required by law or necessary to protect legal rights.

The public list of signatories may be kept as archival material until consent to publication is withdrawn.

7. Protection of information

We use access restrictions, private folders, account controls, and other reasonable organizational and technical measures. At the same time, no method of transmitting or storing information guarantees absolute security.

8. Individual rights

A person may request access to their data, its correction, restriction or termination of processing, deletion, or withdrawal of consent, unless otherwise provided by law. They may also withdraw consent to being publicly named in the list of signatories.

Requests should be sent to office@15057.org.ua. To protect personal data, we may ask the requester to confirm their identity.

9. Children's data

The form is not intended for independent filling by children. Parents and legal guardians should not share the child's medical or other personal data through the form, unless specifically requested to do so on a lawful basis.

10. Technical files and third-party links

The site does not use advertising or behavioral tracking systems. Infrastructure providers may use purely technical files or logs necessary for the security and operation of the service. The site contains links to external resources whose policies are governed by their own rules.

11. Changes to the Policy

The policy may be updated in connection with changes in site functions, organization status or legislation. The current revision is always published on this page with the date of the update.

12. Legal basis

The policy is prepared taking into account the Law of Ukraine “On Personal Data Protection” and the Law of Ukraine “On Information”.
