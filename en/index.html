No. 15057 · Restoring the right to discharge from service

Equal state protection for children with disabilities regardless of their parents’ status

We are working to restore the right to discharge from military service for parents of children with disabilities. We bring families and experts together to secure the passage of Draft Law No. 15057 and equal protection for every child.

Sign the petition Our demand
01 Best interests of the child 02 Legal certainty 03 Non-discrimination
Key principle
A child with a disability must not receive less state protection simply because one parent is already serving in the military.

The problem and the solution · No. 15057

Restoring the right to discharge from military service

A child needs their parents’ care and involvement whether or not either parent serves in the military. The child’s needs must come first.

01

What is wrong today

Parents of children with disabilities are denied discharge because the other parent is present — even when the child needs both parents’ involvement.

02

What needs to change

Children with the same legal status must receive equal state protection and genuine care, not just formal guarantees — regardless of their parents’ status.

03

The solution — Draft Law No. 15057

We are calling for its adoption to restore the right to discharge from military service for parents raising children with disabilities.

About us

We are a community of families and the team behind The Future Has the Right NGO

The Future Has the Right NGO brings together military families raising children with disabilities under 18, professionals and members of the public committed to equal protection of children’s rights.

Our priority is to restore the right to discharge from military service for parents of children with disabilities through the adoption of Draft Law No. 15057. We are non-partisan and base our advocacy on legal, medical, social and expert evidence.

01
FocusBest interests of the child

Equal state protection regardless of the parents’ status.

02
Our approachEvidence, not slogans

Legal, medical, social and expert assessment.

03
Our goalA fair state response

Resolve the legal inconsistency and prevent discrimination against children.
