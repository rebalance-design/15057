Public expert meeting · Draft Law No. 15057

Equal state protection of children with disabilities regardless of the status of their parents

On July 17, 2026, a public discussion of Draft Law No. 15057 was held with the participation of families of service members, human rights, medical, rehabilitation, social and expert communities. As a result of the meeting, we adopted a joint resolution in support of equal state protection of children with disabilities regardless of the status of their parents.

Key principle
A child with a disability must not receive a lower level of state protection just because one of the parents is already a service member.

About us

We are a community of families and the team of NGO “The Future Has the Right”

NGO “The Future Has the Right” unites families of service members who raise minor children with disabilities, as well as specialists and caring citizens who are ready to work for the equal protection of children's rights.

Our primary work focuses on Draft Law No. 15057 and the legal inconsistency that can deprive a child with a disability of an equal level of state protection after one parent is mobilized. We are non-partisan and base our position on legal, medical, social and expert evidence.

Draft law

The essence of Draft Law No. 15057

Draft Law No. 15057 is aimed at eliminating the inconsistency between the rules on the right to deferment for parents of children with disabilities and the right to discharge from military service for those parents who are already serving.

  • the issue of equal state protection of children with disabilities;
  • the issue of the best interests of the child;
  • issues of legal certainty and non-discrimination;
  • a matter of real, not formal care.
